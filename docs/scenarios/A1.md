# Scenario A1: interpretation note for "Make the analytics more accurate"

Branch: `feat/a-analytics-accuracy` (from `main`; not merged). Ambiguous-requirement workflow,
design §10.7 and §11.4.

## Input (deliberately vague)
"Make the analytics more accurate." The acceptance criterion is an adjective, the inputs and
outputs are implied, and the decision owner is unclear, so the ticket is triaged as ambiguous
(§10.7 rubric).

## Candidate interpretations (drafted by the AI)

| # | Interpretation | What changes | Trade-off | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I1 | Exclude bots from headline numbers | `exclude_bots` query parameter on `/stats` | Changing the default would break consumers, so keep it opt-in | S |
| I2 | Better unique-visitor estimate | Distinct `ip_hash` over the whole range instead of a per-day notion | Salt rotation breaks continuity (PRD OQ-5) | S |
| I3 | Fewer lost clicks | Shorter flush interval or a durable queue | A durable queue adds infrastructure (out of scope for the prototype) | M-L |
| I4 | Correct day boundaries for the owner | `tz` query parameter for day buckets | More complex SQL; UTC stays the default (ADR 0007) | M |
| I5 | Fresher counts | Lower the flush interval to 500 ms | More DB writes | S |

## Questions the AI raised for the human
1. Is "accurate" about bots (I1), people (I2), loss (I3), time zones (I4) or freshness (I5)?
2. May the default `/stats` output change? (The AI recommended "no": byte-identical by default.)
3. Is any new infrastructure acceptable? (The AI recommended "no" for the prototype.)

## Outcome (A2)
The engineer chose **I1 + I2**: low risk, testable and backward compatible. I3 and I4 are deferred
to the backlog with reasons, and I5 is not pursued. The decision is recorded in
[ADR 0010](../adr/0010-analytics-accuracy.md); the delivery evidence is in [A3](A3.md).
